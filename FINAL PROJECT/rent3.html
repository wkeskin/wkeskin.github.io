Description

Wonderful rental opportunity! This beautifully maintained 2+1 bedroom detached home with 1.5 baths features beautiful hardwood and thoughtful updates throughout. Main level offers large living room, separate dining room, sunny den, warm updated kitchen with plenty of cupboard space as well as lovely full bath. Retreat to the second level with its 2 sizable bedrooms. Partially finished lower level features sizable bedroom or bonus room (which could also be perfect for a den, office or playroom) as well as partial bathroom, laundry and plenty of convenient space for storage. Spacious, private rear yard with storage shed, deck and patio area - great for summer barbecues. Private driveway but also great for those needing transportation as it’s steps from the Westboro Bus Terminal. Amazing location around the corner from Farm Boy and just minutes to the Superstore and the quaint shops, services & restaurants on Richmond.
